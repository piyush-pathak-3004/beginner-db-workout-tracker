ℹ️ About This Plan

Absolute beginner 4-day split designed for a single pair of 2.5 kg dumbbells. Prioritizes form, safety, habit building, and long-term health (posture, strength, bone density).

Split

  • Day 1: Chest + Triceps
  • Day 2: Back + Biceps
  • Day 3: Rest
  • Day 4: Shoulders + Abs
  • Day 5: Legs + Glutes
  • Day 6–7: Rest

Rules

  • No push-ups
  • ~45 min including warm-up
  • 1 easier alternative for every exercise
  • Higher reps (12–20) + slow tempo because weight is light
  • Stop if pain

💡 How to Use

  1. Pick a day from the dashboard or tabs.
  2. Do the warm-up.
  3. Tap the numbered circles after each set.
  4. When all sets of an exercise are checked, it marks complete.
  5. When every exercise is done, the whole day is marked complete and your streak updates.
  6. Add notes if you want (reps felt easy/hard, form tips, etc.).
